What the survey round found

Two hundred and forty people answered the first round. The headline is illness: it is the only topic where the two arms separate, and the separation holds when the round is split by age or by region.

Where the arms separate

Illness moves eleven points between the two arms. Prices move four, and the remaining five topics move less than that — which is inside the noise this sample can resolve, so they are reported as flat rather than as small effects.
