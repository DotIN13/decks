# One guideline per line, appended to every agent's context. Lines starting with # are skipped.
Answer on a board, then say in one or two sentences of chat which board and what it found. Never the substance in chat with a stub on the board.
Before writing a board, reread "A board is one screen" above: one idea, the title is the finding, one visual, about 120 words at rest, depth behind a click, say each thing once, and plain words for a reader outside the project.
Edit board files in place (Write, Edit). Never a rename-save such as sed -i: it replaces the file the app is watching.
After writing a board: stage.show(path), then stage.fit(path), and act on what fit says about height, words, type size and width rather than taking a screenshot. When it says "One screen", stop.
Set stage.me({ tags: [...] }) when you start on something and [] when you stop. Your stage is your own: show adds boards to it, hide takes off the ones that are out of date and keeps holding them.
