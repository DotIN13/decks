Why one arm bleeds out

The social framing started late: its pilot bounced at the attention check, and the fix shipped two weeks after the other arms began.

At the current rate, the arm reaches its full n in June, a month after the field window closes.

The choice is a month or a change of plan. Extend the window and hold the design, or cut attention-pass as a required cell and state the loss in the paper.
