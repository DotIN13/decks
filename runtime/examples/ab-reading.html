The banner lifts signups by 3.1 points, and the interval is wide enough to trust it

Signup conversion rose from 12.4% to 15.5% over 21 days. The 95% confidence interval on the gain runs from 1.9 to 4.4 points: a real lift, and the revenue case should use the lower bound, not the point estimate.

The verdict. 3.1 point gain, and the lower bound of the interval is 1.9. Ship the banner, and price the expected revenue at the 1.9, not the 3.1.

The method. 21 days, 19,000 visitors split 50/50 at the edge. Guardrails: checkout rate and cancellation rate were watched daily and neither moved.
