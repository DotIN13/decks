3,400 checkouts failed between 17:42 and 18:37. The retry storm staved off a service outage for the first 17 minutes, and the alert that should have fired at 17:50 did not arrive until 17:58.

Where the 8 minutes went

The error budget rule only watches the payments gateway, and this failure was downstream of it.

The rollback went out at 18:11 to prove the point: the previous release was green, and everything that followed was its own retry loop.
