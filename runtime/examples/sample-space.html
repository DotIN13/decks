The two arms separate in the first two dimensions, and the third is one clinic's

240 respondents, embedded into three dimensions: the waitlist arm separates cleanly from control, and the discount arm sits between them. The third dimension, the one the plot is built on, explains 9% of the variance and is almost entirely one recruiting clinic.

The sample in the space the survey found

Drag to turn the space. Hover a point for the respondent beneath it. The axes are the first three principal components of the survey responses.

How to read it

Each point is one respondent. The distances are the model's own: responses that agree sit together, and an arm that is its own cloud is an arm the survey is measuring.

Points were picked at random; the arms have equal sample sizes by design.
