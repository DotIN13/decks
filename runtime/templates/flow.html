Repeat visits fell 11 points after the second tab shipped. The fall is steady month to month and shows on both desktop and mobile, so it is not one bad week.

Summary

We counted unique visitors per month for one store and split them into first visits and repeat visits. Repeat visits fell from 42% in January to 31% in December. First visits rose by 9 points over the same twelve months.

Method

Monthly unique visitors, one store, split by first visit and repeat visit. A visitor is a repeat if the store had seen them before that month.

Result

Repeat visits fell 11 points. The fall is steady across the year rather than a single bad month, and it is slightly larger on desktop than on mobile.

select month, count(distinct visitor_id) filter (where prior_visits > 0) as repeat_visits
from visits group by month order by month;
